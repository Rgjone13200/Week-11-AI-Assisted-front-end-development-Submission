EcoRise mission

EcoRise is a Seattle-based nonprofit dedicated to empowering people and neighborhoods to live more sustainably through education, hands-on experiences, and community-led action.

Local impact

Together we co-host workshops, support volunteer projects, and share resources that help neighborhoods reduce waste, conserve energy, and build healthier local ecosystems.
